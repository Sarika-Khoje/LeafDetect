About LeafLense AI

A Computer Vision and Machine Learning system for tomato plant disease detection.

🔬 How It Works

  1. Preprocessing — Images are resized to 128×128 px, normalised, and converted to RGB, HSV, and LAB colour spaces.
  2. Feature Extraction — Colour histograms, colour statistics, GLCM texture, LBP histograms, and Hu moments are extracted (~530 features).
  3. PCA — Principal Component Analysis reduces features to ~95% explained variance, removing redundancy.
  4. Classification — Three models are trained and compared: KNN, SVM (RBF kernel), and Random Forest.

🤖 Models Compared

ModelStrengths
KNN Simple, intuitive, no training phase. Uses distance-weighted voting.
SVM Effective in high-dimensional spaces. RBF kernel handles non-linear boundaries.
RF Ensemble of decision trees. Robust to overfitting, handles class imbalance.
